## #331 — remove finalize()
- **Proposed in #331** ("remove finalize()"). Not progressed to removal of the safety-net finalisers.
- **Done (deliberate partial):** a full finaliser inventory (3 sites); a `--finalization=disabled` probe
  proving the flag disables finalize and the happy-path tests still pass; javadoc on each safety net
  explaining WHY it is retained and that it must move to `Cleaner`, not be dropped (`e6c29286`).
- **Deliberately NOT done:** removing the safety-net finalisers (`AbstractCloseable.Finalizer`,
  `CleaningRandomAccessFile`) — retained, because their value is the un-happy path (a production caller
  that forgot to close leaks a descriptor) which tests do not cover; the actual `Cleaner` re-homing
  (future, gated on real finalize removal); replacing the test-only detector
  `CloseableUtils.gcAndWaitForCloseablesToClose()` (a lower-urgency test-infra task).
- **Changes the verdict:** nothing changes the retention; the JDK actually removing finalization only
  changes *timing* — it promotes the Cleaner re-homing to "do now" (behaviour preserved, deprecated
  `finalize()` then deleted).
